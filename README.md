## 💡 About
An interactive web application and risk assessment console designed for astronauts to monitor health indicators, understand physiological changes, and manage risks associated with the five primary hazards of human spaceflight: **Space Radiation**, **Isolation and Confinement**, **Distance from Earth**, **Gravity Fields**, and **Hostile/Closed Environments**[cite: 1, 2]. Built for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge**[cite: 1].

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Multi-Environment Simulation:** Adapt health protocols across LEO Station (ISS), Deep Space Transit, Lunar Base, and Mars Surface Base.
- **Interactive Body Map:** Clickable anatomical system views (Cardiovascular, Neurological, Musculoskeletal, etc.) with real-time health status and microgravity self-exam guidelines.
- **Biometric Self-Assessment & Telemetry:** Track vital signs, simulate anomalies, and follow step-by-step measurement protocols designed for non-medical crew members.
- **Crew Gamification:** Daily health routines, XP leveling, and engagement badges to maintain physical and mental well-being on long-duration missions.
